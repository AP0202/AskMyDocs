SECTION: Test Summary
This report contains the results of a Complete Blood Count (CBC) with differential,
a common blood test that checks the levels of red blood cells, white blood cells,
and platelets. It is often used as a general health screen and can help detect
conditions such as anemia, infection, and clotting problems.

SECTION: White Blood Cell (WBC) Count
Result: 11.8 x10^3/uL
Reference Range: 4.0 - 11.0 x10^3/uL
Flag: HIGH
Plain-language note: White blood cells help your body fight infection. A value
above the normal range can happen with infections, inflammation, or stress on
the body, but can also be a temporary finding. It is not a diagnosis by itself.

SECTION: Interpretation Notes from Lab
The combination of an elevated WBC count with elevated neutrophils is a pattern
sometimes seen during a bacterial infection or significant inflammation. This
lab report does not include a diagnosis. Please discuss these results with your
ordering provider, especially if you have symptoms such as fever, pain, or
fatigue.
