ATTENDING PHYSICIAN: Dr. M. Okafor, Hospital Medicine

SECTION: Reason for Admission
Patient was admitted with fever, productive cough, and shortness of breath.
Chest imaging and clinical exam were consistent with community-acquired
pneumonia affecting the right lower lobe.

SECTION: Hospital Course
Patient was started on intravenous antibiotics (ceftriaxone) and supportive
care including supplemental oxygen. Fever resolved by hospital day 2. Oxygen
levels normalized by hospital day 3, and the patient was transitioned to an
oral antibiotic (amoxicillin-clavulanate) prior to discharge. Patient
tolerated the transition well with no recurrence of fever or breathing
difficulty.

SECTION: Discharge Diagnosis
1. Community-acquired pneumonia, right lower lobe, resolving.
2. Mild dehydration, resolved with IV fluids.

SECTION: Discharge Medications
1. Amoxicillin-clavulanate 875mg/125mg, by mouth, twice daily, for 5 more
   days (complete the full course even if feeling better).
2. Acetaminophen 500mg, by mouth, every 6 hours as needed for fever or pain.
3. Continue home medications as previously prescribed (see home medication
   list from primary care provider).

SECTION: Follow-up Instructions
1. Follow up with primary care provider within 5-7 days of discharge.
2. Return to the emergency department if you experience worsening shortness
   of breath, chest pain, fever above 101.5F (38.6C), confusion, or bluish
   discoloration of lips or fingertips.
3. Repeat chest imaging may be recommended in 6 weeks if symptoms persist,
   to confirm the pneumonia has fully cleared.
4. Rest and stay hydrated. Avoid strenuous activity for at least one week.
